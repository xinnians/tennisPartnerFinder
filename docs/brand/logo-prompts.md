# 第一輪 Logo 概念 prompts

共通要求：概念板只用來比較策略，不是 final artwork。採用現有 `#12291c`、`#1c5c3c`、`#ddf53c`、`#faf9f3`；扁平、向量友善、單色仍成立，避免交叉球拍、盾牌、皇冠、火焰、人物剪影、擬真球、3D、漸層與 mockup。

## 揪拍

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: first-round identity concept sheet for a mobile-first Taipei tennis session-finding product
Primary request: create a restrained, original logo system for the Traditional Chinese name "揪拍". The concept should express inviting a tennis partner and two sides meeting through a ball exchange. Explore one strong symbol through three controlled variations: two converging court or ball-trajectory arcs, a compact paired form, and a map-aware form without using a generic location pin.
Style/medium: flat vector-like geometry, bold silhouette, modern Taiwanese sports utility rather than a club crest
Composition/framing: clean 2x2 presentation on warm off-white; symbol, horizontal lockup, monochrome test, and 24 px app-icon test
Color palette: deep ink green #12291c, court green #1c5c3c, signal tennis yellow #ddf53c, warm off-white #faf9f3
Text (verbatim): "揪拍"
Constraints: render only the exact two Traditional Chinese characters; keep forms legible and suitable for later manual vector redraw
Avoid: crossed rackets, shields, crowns, flames, player silhouettes, photorealistic tennis balls, gradients, 3D, mockups, watermark, extra text
```
