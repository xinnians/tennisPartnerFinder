# 球咖第一輪 ImageGen prompts

每個資產使用一次獨立的內建 ImageGen 呼叫；沒有使用 API／CLI fallback。

## 2D 咖杯 flat mark

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: first-round flat logo mark for "球咖" (Qiu-Ka), a friendly Taiwanese tennis matchmaking app for finding tennis buddies nearby
Primary request: a tennis ball floating just above a coffee cup, with at most two subtle steam strokes. The cup and ball must read immediately as one compact logo silhouette.
Scene/backdrop: one perfectly uniform solid warm cream #F7F1E2 background edge to edge
Style/medium: minimal flat 2D vector-like geometry, modern Japanese-Taiwanese cafe branding, one consistent stroke weight, rounded corners, optical balance
Composition/framing: centered mark filling about 55% of a square canvas with generous negative space
Color palette: court green cup #2E6B40, tennis-ball orange #E2762C, cream seam lines #F7F1E2, optional deep ink green #1C2A1F details
Text: none
Constraints: the ball has exactly two symmetric curved tennis seam lines; crisp hard edges; readable at 48 px; warm, playful community-club feel, not corporate and not aggressive
Avoid: text, letters, watermark, photorealism, busy background, gradients, shadows, lighting effects, extra objects, saucer, racket, clipart look, 3D bevel, plastic look
```
